CURRENT CITY LEADERSHIP

Marjorie "Joie" Piczon Perales

Marjorie "Joie" Piczon Perales is a Filipino public servant and healthcare advocate currently serving as the City Mayor of Toledo City, Cebu. Affectionately known as "Mayor Joie," she is affiliated with the 1Cebu political party and secured her mayoral post through significant victories, including winning 56.21% of the vote (72,616 votes) in the 2025 local elections.

As a local executive, Mayor Perales has blended her background as a professional nurse with responsive, innovative public administration, earning her reputation as a highly visible, welfare-driven local chief executive.

LEADERSHIP TIMELINE

Mayors of Toledo

01

Professional Background & Entry to Politics

Before entering politics, Mayor Perales practiced as a licensed nurse. This medical background anchors her leadership philosophy, giving her a hands-on approach to managing local healthcare systems, community wellness, and emergency crises.

02

Key Policies & Governance Innovations

The 4-Day Compressed Workweek: Implemented for Toledo City government offices (Mondays through Thursdays, 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM) while exempting critical frontliners, disaster response, and health services.

Healthcare Modernization: Lobbied for a ₱10-million budget allocation to rehabilitate and modernize the Toledo City Hospital, alongside upgrading the city's emergency fleet with fully operational response medical vehicles.

Empowerment through Local Skills Training: Partnered with the CSWDO and institutions like Cebu Technological University (CTU) to provide free skills training (tailoring, dressmaking, and food processing) to women's organizations across Toledo.

03

Awards and Public Recognition

Top Performing Mayor: Ranked 2nd Top Performing Mayor in Cebu Province by RPMD Foundation Inc. with an 87.8% approval rating.

Social Welfare Laurels: Honored as Outstanding City Mayor in the Visayas by social work associations.

National Honors: Received accolades at the Nation Builders and Mosliv Awards at Okada Manila and the GSIS Protection Award for outstanding local asset protection and risk management.

Leadership Philosophy: "It is not I, but we" - emphasizing collaboration with Vice Mayor Jay Sigue and the Toledo City Council.
