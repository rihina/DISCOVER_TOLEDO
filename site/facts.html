CITY PROFILE

Toledo at a glance.

Toledo (tə-LED-oh), in Cebuano Dakbayan sa Toledo, is a component city in Cebu province. It is named after Toledo, Spain, and its patron saint is John of Sahagun.

CLIMATE

Warm all year, wet from May.

Highs stay between 28 and 31 °C. Rain falls on about 253 days a year, with June to October the wettest months. Source: Meteoblue.

ECONOMY

Copper city, power city.

Toledo is a 1st class city by income. Its 2022 revenue was ₱2,198 million against ₱1,789 million in expenditure, with ₱6,960 million in assets. Poverty incidence was 32.38% in 2021.

Poverty incidence over time

It dropped to 17.20% in 2018, then rose to 32.38% in 2021.

GEOGRAPHY

Where Toledo sits.

Toledo is the only Cebu city on the western seaboard, facing Negros Oriental across the Tañon Strait. It sits at 10°23′N 123°39′E, rising from sea level to 981 m.

PEOPLE & LEARNING

Notable Toledanos.

Toledo City Science High School, founded in 1996 in Ilihan Heights, is the city’s DepEd-recognized science high school.
